# Rural Market Accessibility Analysis

## Part 1: The question
Which settlements in Obafemi Owode Local Government Area, Ogun State, are located more than 5 kilometres from a recognized marketplace?

## Part 2: Why it matters
This analysis identifies isolated communities that face severe barriers to selling agricultural goods and buying daily necessities. Local government planners and agricultural NGOs can use this to prioritize where to build new rural market hubs or where to improve feeder roads.

## Part 3 and 4: The data needed and sources
* Marketplaces (Amenity points) - OSM via Geofabrik - https://download.geofabrik.de/africa/nigeria.html - Shapefile (.shp.zip) - ~200 MB
* Road networks - OSM via Geofabrik - https://download.geofabrik.de/africa/nigeria.html - Shapefile (.shp.zip) - ~200 MB
* Settlement extents - GRID3 - https://data.grid3.org/ - GeoPackage - ~40 MB
* Local government area boundaries - Humanitarian Data Exchange (HDX) - https://data.humdata.org/ - Shapefile (.shp.zip) - ~10 MB

## Part 5: What you would build
An interactive web map that local agricultural extension officers can open to instantly view the transport isolation of any farming settlement, helping them target communities needing mobile markets or transport assistance.
